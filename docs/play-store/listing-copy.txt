APP NAME (30)
JyutTranslate

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max)
Live English ↔ Cantonese: voice, camera OCR, and documents with Jyutping.

FULL DESCRIPTION
JyutTranslate helps you move between English and Hong Kong Cantonese (粵語) in real life — not textbook Mandarin.

Speak, type, or point your camera. Get clear Cantonese with Jyutping so you can pronounce what you see and hear.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Live voice translate — English ↔ Cantonese, hands-free conversation mode
• Camera translate — snap menus, signs, and labels; read them in your language
• Documents — translate PDFs and office files while keeping layout where possible
• Jyutping — romanization to learn tones and pronunciation
• Character breakdown — tap Chinese to explore meanings and readings
• Text-to-speech — hear natural Cantonese playback

WHO IT’S FOR
Travelers, families, learners, and anyone who needs practical Hong Kong Cantonese — at the table, on the street, or on a call.

PLANS
Free to try. Family and Business plans unlock more live minutes, camera time, and document pages.

Privacy-minded: we process speech, photos, and text to deliver your translation. We don’t sell your personal information. Details: https://www.jyuttranslate.com (Privacy Policy).
