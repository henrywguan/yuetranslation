# Request account & data deletion

**App / service:** JyutTranslate (https://www.jyuttranslate.com)  
**Developer / operator:** Henry Guan, doing business as JyutTranslate  
**Last updated:** September 5, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your **JyutTranslate** account and associated personal data. Use this URL for Google Play Data safety and other store listings that require an account-deletion request link.

---

## How to request deletion

Account deletion is handled by email (self-serve deletion is not yet available in the app).

1. Send an email to **[henrywguan@gmail.com](mailto:henrywguan@gmail.com?subject=JyutTranslate%20account%20deletion%20request)**.
2. Use the **subject line:** `JyutTranslate account deletion request`.
3. Include:
   - The **email address** on your JyutTranslate account
   - Confirmation that you want to **delete the account and associated personal data**
4. We may reply to verify that you control the account (for example, by confirming from the same email address).
5. After verification, we process the request within a **reasonable period** (typically within **30 days**).

**Quick link:** [Email a deletion request](mailto:henrywguan@gmail.com?subject=JyutTranslate%20account%20deletion%20request)

You can also start from Contact in the site footer (`mailto:henrywguan@gmail.com`) and use the same subject line.

---

## What we delete

When we approve an account deletion request, we delete or de-identify account-linked personal data we control, including where applicable:

- Account and profile information (email, auth identifiers, optional username, saved voice preferences, plan/status flags)
- Household membership and invite records tied to your account (and related household records when you are the only remaining member / owner, as applicable)
- Usage / entitlement meters linked to your account (and household pooled meters where they exist solely for that household)
- Bug reports you submitted (including optional screenshots/notes)
- Admin/security records we are not legally required to retain

Translation text, microphone audio, photos, and documents submitted for translation are **not** kept by us as a lasting content archive after processing; there is no separate “translation history” database to delete beyond ephemeral technical handling.

---

## What we may keep (and for how long)

We may retain certain information after account deletion when required or permitted:

| Data | Typical retention |
| --- | --- |
| **Stripe** payment / invoice / subscription records | Per Stripe’s practices and legal/accounting requirements (often years for tax and dispute purposes) |
| Records needed for **legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax, or accounting** obligations | As long as required by law or necessary for those purposes |
| **Residual backups** | Limited time until rotated out of backup systems |
| **Anonymized / aggregated** analytics or operational metrics that no longer identify you | May be retained |

If you only want specific personal data deleted **without** closing the account, email [henrywguan@gmail.com](mailto:henrywguan@gmail.com) with that request. We will evaluate it under applicable law and our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

---

## Paid subscriptions

Deleting your account does **not** automatically cancel a Stripe subscription. To avoid future charges, cancel through the in-app billing / Stripe Customer Portal (or ask us in the same email). Paid access generally continues until the end of the current billing period after cancellation, as described in our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md).
