## Required packs (task)

### `adventurers/`

- **Source used:** itch Free 2.0 (`https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers`)
- **Upload:** Free 2.0 (12 MB) — Extra 2.0 / Source Files 2.0 **not** taken (paid)
- **Contents:** Characters, props, textures, sample GLB/FBX + included free animations subset
- **License:** `adventurers/License.txt` (CC0)
- **Fallback not used:** GitHub 1.0 zipball available; itch 2.0 preferred and obtained

### `character-animations/`

- **Source used:** itch Free 1.1 (`https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-character-animations`)
- **Upload:** Free 1.1 (14 MB) — Extra tier **not** taken
- **Contents:** Rig_Medium / Rig_Large animation GLBs + FBX, mannequin characters
- **License:** `character-animations/License.txt` (CC0)
- **Fallback not used:** OpenGameArt `kaykit_character_animations_1.2.zip` available; itch 1.1 preferred and obtained
