# HarborRPG · The Tide That Remembers

**IP:** Original Harbor / Jade worldbuilding  
**Tone inspirations (systems only):** Black Desert Online · Elder Scrolls Online · MapleStory  

HarborRPG is **not** a Cantonese classroom. Story lives in dungeon instances, quest blurbs, and boss phase beats.

## Premise

The ferry chronometers that once kept every pier in the same hour cracked on the same night. Three sealed places woke. Crossroads Town still sells salves and lists parties — but sailors start forgetting the names of docks they slept at yesterday.

You are an adventurer with a soft class kit and a harder question: **who is rewriting the Harbor’s memory, and why does the tide answer when you speak?**

## Inspiration map (do not copy plots)

| Source feel | Harbor original |
|---|---|
| BDO — calamity stones, guild wars over trade routes | **Black Tide Pearl** sunk in Tide Hollow; first ferry guilds bled for docking rights |
| ESO — ancient vaults, planar / daedric pressure | **Chronicle Vault** — plane-thin ledgers; ink that invites an Other-side reader |
| Maple — adventure isles, mirrored / corrupted maps | **Echo Isle** — kinder twin at the mirrored pier; only one voyage survives |

Ash Crypt (already shipped) is the prologue dungeon: embers of a war with no victors.

## Chapter spine

1. **Ash Remembers** (`crypt`) — Ash Warden. Learn that ash is memory that refuses to cool.
2. **Black Tide Hollow** (`tidehollow`) — Pearl Host. Calamity pearl hungers for **names**; phases strip buffs / raise threat / summon tide thralls.
3. **Chronicle Vault** (`chronicle`) — Ink Archivist. Ledgers rewrite mid-fight; phases open rift adds and silence windows.
4. **Echo Isle** (`echoisle`) — Mirror Ferry. Meet your twin; final phase forces a choice beat (soft: twin enrages if you hesitate / heal selfishly).
5. **Tide Remembers** (`tideraid`) — three-wing raid: Tide Herald → Depth Archivist → Tide Sovereign. Heroic recommended.

## Party & Realtime

Story assumes soft multiplayer: remotes visible in-zone, party invites on the Town finder (tank/heal/dps roles), contested loot after boss kills. No anti-cheat host.

## Feel bar

Boss phase transitions must be **immediate and readable** (glow, toast, attack cadence). Flag Henry before cutting phase FX for lean pipelines.
