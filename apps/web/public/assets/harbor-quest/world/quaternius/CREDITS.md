Textures are resized to 512 WebP. The paid MegaKit Pro/Source zips were not used.

Google Drive was still over its public download quota for Ultimate Fantasy RTS, Ultimate Modular Ruins, Ultimate Crops, the small Ships pack, and Ultimate RPG. Those full folders are not in this set. Pirate Kit covers the dock, ships, and a handful of item props. Modular Dungeons covers instance arches.
